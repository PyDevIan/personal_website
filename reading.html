How I Work

A practical approach to business discovery, architecture, implementation, validation, and operational adoption.

  1. 01

    Understand the business problem

    Start with the people, workflow, and decision the application needs to support. Clarify requirements, constraints, available data, and what a useful result looks like before choosing a technical solution.

    Focus: A clear problem, agreed scope, and practical acceptance criteria.

  2. 02

    Choose the right architecture

    Evaluate feasibility and build-vs-buy options. Define service boundaries, data flows, integrations, and access controls. Choose AI where it adds value, with explicit responsibilities for the application, the model, and the user.

    Focus: An architecture that fits the workflow and its operational constraints.

  3. 03

    Build a focused solution

    Translate the architecture into working services and interfaces. Keep extraction, retrieval, model interaction, validation, and enterprise integration understandable and testable. Use reusable application patterns where they reduce complexity.

    Focus: A working application with clear contracts and maintainable components.

  4. 04

    Validate behavior and keep people in control

    Check representative inputs and failure cases, including incomplete documents, missing information, and unexpected model outputs. Use structured outputs, validation rules, and human review where decisions require oversight.

    Focus: Defined checks, visible uncertainty, and a path for review or escalation.

  5. 05

    Prepare for production

    Treat authentication, authorization, testing, deployment, and observability as part of delivery. Make failures diagnosable through health checks, structured logs, metrics, and tracing. Keep exports and automated actions auditable.

    Focus: Operational visibility and clear controls around application behavior.

  6. 06

    Support adoption and improvement

    Coordinate stakeholders, contributors, and partners throughout delivery. Clarify how people will use and support the application, document important decisions, and use operational feedback to prioritize the next improvement.

    Focus: Shared ownership, usable documentation, and a practical improvement path.
