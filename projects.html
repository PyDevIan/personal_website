AI-Assisted Invoice Processing & ERP Integration

Problem:

Mixed Greek and English invoices arrive as PDFs and images with varied layouts. Manual transcription into Excel and ERP workflows is time-consuming, error-prone, and difficult to audit.

Solution:

I designed and delivered an AI-assisted document processing workflow combining PDF extraction, OCR fallback, LLM-based structured data normalization, human-in-the-loop validation, duplicate prevention, and auditable export controls for ERP workflows.

  1. Extract embedded PDF text, falling back to layout-aware OCR and targeted field corrections when needed.
  2. Normalize invoice fields into a strict schema, with date, amount, and VAT checks. An optional evaluator compares extraction results against ground truth.
  3. Let authorized users review and correct fields alongside a PDF preview in a Streamlit validator.
  4. Export only validated, not-yet-exported rows to the ERP import format, recording user and date stamps to support duplicate prevention and auditability.

Human review controls which records are exported for ERP import.

Fridge Alert Notification System

Problem:

Industrial temperature alerts need timely acknowledgment, a clear owner, and reliable follow-up to support food-storage operations.

Solution:

I developed an automated industrial monitoring workflow that processes alert emails from fridge recorders and sends Microsoft Teams notifications to the responsible operational roles.

A FastAPI-based acknowledgment interface lets technicians claim or acknowledge an alert and records their response. Unacknowledged alerts trigger escalation and follow-up notifications until a matching resolution email identifies the device alert as inactive.

Structured CSV logs record alerts, acknowledgments, and responsible personnel, supporting auditability and operational reliability.
