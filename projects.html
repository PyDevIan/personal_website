01 / LLM application

On-Premises HR Knowledge Assistant

RAG & LLM Integration

Problem

Employees need convenient access to internal information and HR policies, with a structured route for requests the assistant cannot resolve.

My role

02 / Document AI

AI-Assisted Invoice Processing

Document Validation & ERP Integration

Problem

Greek and English invoices arrive as PDFs and images with varied layouts. Manual transcription into Excel and ERP workflows is slow, error-prone, and difficult to audit.

My role

Designed and delivered the extraction, validation, and controlled export workflow.

Key engineering decisions

  • Prefer embedded PDF text; fall back to layout-aware OCR and targeted field corrections.
  • Normalize fields into a strict schema with date, amount, and VAT checks; use an optional ground-truth evaluator.
  • Let authorized users review and correct data alongside PDF previews in a Streamlit validator.
  • Export only validated, not-yet-exported rows and record user/date stamps.

Delivered capability

A reviewable invoice-to-ERP workflow with structured data, human approval, duplicate-prevention controls, and an auditable export trail.

03 / Workflow automation

Fridge Alert Notification System

Industrial Monitoring & Operational Automation

Problem

Industrial temperature alerts require timely acknowledgment, clear responsibility, and reliable follow-up to support food-storage operations.

My role

Developed the email-processing workflow, Teams notifications, and FastAPI acknowledgment interface.

Key engineering decisions

  • Process alert emails from industrial fridge recorders and notify responsible roles in Microsoft Teams.
  • Provide a FastAPI interface for technicians to claim or acknowledge an alert.
  • Escalate unanswered alerts and follow up until a matching resolution email marks the device alert inactive.
  • Log alerts, acknowledgments, and responsible personnel in structured CSV records.

Delivered capability

An automated notification workflow with acknowledgment tracking, follow-up logic, and auditability for operational teams.
